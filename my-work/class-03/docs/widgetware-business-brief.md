# WidgetWare Business Brief

WidgetWare sells software and services that help manufacturing and industrial-automation companies modernize plant operations and adopt AI-enabled automation.

## 1. Offerings
- **Plant Operations Platform**: centralizing real-time machinery monitoring and data ingestion via standard industrial protocols (Modbus, OPC UA).
- **Industrial AI Accelerator**: enabling predictive maintenance and quality control by running machine learning models on edge devices or in the cloud.

## 2. Target Market
- **Preferred Industries**: Manufacturing, Industrial Automation, Automotive Manufacturing, Aerospace Manufacturing, Chemical Manufacturing.
- **Excluded Industries**: Software, Retail, Financial Services, Healthcare Providers, Professional Services.
- **Preferred Regions**: North America, Europe.
- **Minimum Scale**: Companies with at least 100 employees.

## 3. Engagement Boundaries
A future WidgetWare SDR agent must:
- Stop before performing any external action.
- Require human approval before sending emails, social messages, or modifying CRM data.
- Make no contractual or pricing commitments.
